What this is. Bitcoin puzzle #71 is a wallet whose creator deliberately used a private key inside a known 71-bit range. Anyone who finds it keeps the coins. Ours is the slowest kind of search: a free cloud CPU trying random keys.

What this is not. Nothing here is for sale. There are no tickets, no entries, no stake, and no payout to anybody but the machine's owner. Nobody is being asked for money, and no one who watches this page is owed anything.

The math, plainly. At the rate above, the expected time to find the key is longer than the age of the Earth. The keys scrolling past are a live sample, not the whole story: highlighted lines are real batch starting points from the engine, the rest are drawn from the same range to show you the shape of the haystack. The counter is real.
